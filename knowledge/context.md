# Business context

Agents read this before writing briefs, angles or copy. Replace the example with your own.

## Brand

- Voice: plain, specific, warm. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
- Claims we can make: _list substantiated claims with their source_.
- Claims we must not make: _e.g. health outcomes, guaranteed savings_.

## Customers

- Primary persona: _who, what they're trying to get done, what they've tried_.
- Objections we hear: _price, time, trust…_

## Constraints

- Markets and languages: _e.g. US/en first_.
- AI-generated content disclosure: check each platform's labelling rules before launch.
