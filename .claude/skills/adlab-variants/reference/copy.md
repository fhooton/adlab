# Copy and hook guidance

- The first 1–3 seconds decide stage 1 (hook rate = 3-second views / impressions). Lead with the
  sharpest version of the idea; no logo intros.
- One idea per variant. If you want to test two ideas, make two variants against the same control.
- Specific beats clever: numbers, concrete moments ("6pm panic"), named objects.
- Never assert personal attributes ("Are you overweight?", "You're in debt") — Meta prohibits it.
- Avoid unqualified guarantees, cure claims, before/after framing for health, and ALL CAPS.
- Meta truncates headlines after ~40 characters and primary text after ~125 on most placements.
- Keep captions inside safe zones; templates already reserve the top 14% and bottom 22% on 9:16.
